# Clipzi

[Clipzi](https://clipzi.app) turns long videos (podcasts, interviews, streams, lectures) into finished short vertical clips: it finds the moments that stand on their own, frames each one for vertical (the person talking, a split screen when two talk), captions it, removes dead air and renders the mp4.

This plugin gives Claude two things:

- **The `clipzi` skill**: when to use Clipzi and how, from one request to finished clips.
- **Clipzi's MCP server** (`https://clipzi.app/mcp`): the tools that work on your Clipzi account. You sign in with your Clipzi account the first time; it is created right there if you do not have one, with no card.

## Try it

Ask for what you want in plain words:

- "Make 3 clips with the best moments of this video: (a link to the file, or a Google Drive or Dropbox share link)"
- "From my last video, make 3 clips about pricing."
- "Put our handle at the bottom of yesterday's clips and make them again."

## Plans
